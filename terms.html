Purchase information

Last updated: 2026-10-05. Apple’s purchase confirmation determines the price, currency, billing period, availability and trial eligibility. Prices vary by storefront.

Korean App Store prices

Monthly: KRW 1,900/month. Annual: KRW 14,000/year, charged as one annual total, not monthly installments. Lifetime: KRW 29,000 once, a separate non-consumable purchase.

Seven-day subscription trial

Available for eligible monthly and annual subscriptions. It starts only after choosing and confirming a subscription with Apple, not on installation. Eligibility depends on the Apple account and introductory offers previously used in the subscription group. RapidCat shows a trial only when the actual StoreKit offer and account eligibility are confirmed. Lifetime has no trial.

Automatic renewal and cancellation

Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled. After the trial, Apple charges the displayed price for your selected period. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends to avoid a charge. Manage or cancel in Mac App Store → Account → Subscriptions. Removing the app or stopping Helper does not cancel a subscription. Remaining access follows Apple’s subscription/trial status.

Separate lifetime purchase

Lifetime grants RapidCat Pro access with no scheduled expiration or recurring billing; it does not promise every future separate app or perpetual services. Buying lifetime does not cancel an existing subscription or refund previous charges. Cancel your subscription through Apple to avoid duplicate billing. Refunds or revocation can change access.

Restore and refunds

Restore Purchases with the purchasing Apple account. Access requires verified StoreKit transactions. Request a refund through Apple Report a Problem; Apple determines eligibility and approval.

Helper, data and license

A purchase does not install Helper or grant system permissions. Cancellation or expiration does not delete local clipboard data, settings or models. The App Store app is licensed under Apple’s Standard EULA. This information does not limit statutory consumer rights.
